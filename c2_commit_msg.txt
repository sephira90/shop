refactor(users): relocate Auth + Account slice into Domains/Users (C2)

Auth + Account Orders bounded context into app/Domains/Users/* with
namespace moves and zero dual-namespace shims. Second runtime module
move after C1 (Catalog); exit target #26 fully achieved.
